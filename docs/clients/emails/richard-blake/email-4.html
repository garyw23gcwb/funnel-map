There are two stories people will offer you about your family.

One says you must be a monster. Your child left, so you must have done something terrible.

The other says you did nothing at all, and your child was taken from you.

Both stories are simple. Your life isn't.

Cut Off ends on a third account.

You are not the whole explanation. You may be part of it. Both things can be true.

You are allowed to hold the more complicated account.

Most of the book is about the first half of that. How ordinary family difficulty came to be described in the language of harm. Why bad parenting accounts for far less of it than the culture assumes.

The second half matters too.

In Chapter 1 I wrote that some people will use the book's arguments as cover for behaviour they should, in fact, reflect on. I asked them not to.

One of the hardest things in life is knowing which advice applies to you and which to discard.

My course is built on the same honesty. It isn't for every family. If you were genuinely abusive to your child, it isn't your path, and I say so in the first video.

That's why Video 1 is free. You'll know whether the rest is for you before you've spent anything.
